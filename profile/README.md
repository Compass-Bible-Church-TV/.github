# CBCTV Technology & Media Team

## Mission

The Technology & Media Team exists to glorify God as we support and amplify the impact of Compass Bible Church's 24/7 mission to **Reach, Teach, and Train** disciples of Christ.

## Guiding values

- **8 Distinctives:** Always seeking to stir up the congregation to love and good works by emphasizing, reinforcing, and living out our 8 Distinctives.
- **World-Class Standards:** Always seeking to produce world-class live events and on-demand digital content.
- **Frictionless Access (ATAPAD):** Always seeking to deliver easy access to content any time, any place, any device.
- **Congregational Experience (CX):** Always seeking to uphold a consistent and familiar congregational experience as marked by CBCTV's design aesthetics.
- **Contribution as Worship:** Always seeking to foster open, collaborative, and scalable systems that tap into congregant expertise and provide service opportunities.

## Strategic pillars

1. **Live Event and Studio Production:** Audio, video, lighting, and stage management for weekend services, mid-week ministries, special events, and other creative media initiatives.
2. **Media Pipelines & Distribution:** Video-on-demand (VOD), live-streaming infrastructure, post-production workflows, media asset management, and digital content delivery systems.
3. **Software & Infrastructure:** Software development and support, network engineering, church management systems, IT infrastructure, and data security.

## How we build software

- **Shared standards:** every app follows our product standards, engineering standards and design system, so the congregation meets one consistent experience wherever they find us.
- **Small, safe changes:** short-lived branches, reviewed pull requests, and versioned releases that can be rolled back.
- **Accessible on every device:** usable by keyboard and screen reader, on phones as well as desktops.
- **People's information protected:** personal information stays in the church's systems of record, and public pages show only what's meant to be public.
- **AI as a careful helper:** we build with Claude, guided by the same written standards our people follow, and people make the decisions.
- **How we build software:** [the overview](https://github.com/Compass-Bible-Church-TV/cbc-how-we-build) of how product, design and engineering fit together (members only).
